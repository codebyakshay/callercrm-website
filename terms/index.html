Summary

  • Each Organisation can use one free 14-day trial with Pro features and up to 12 callers. No payment details are needed.

Billing & payment

  • Plans are paid in advance for each month or year. Annual plans are charged at 10 months' price for 12 months.

  • Extra caller packs added in the middle of a billing period are charged for that period.
  • If a renewal isn't paid within 7 days of its due date, the account becomes read-only until it is paid.
  • Payments already made are not refunded, except where the law requires it or where we could not provide the service because of our own fault.

WhatsApp on Pro

  • On Pro, CallerCRM connects to your Organisation's own WhatsApp Business number. We help you set it up.
  • WhatsApp is provided by Meta. You must follow Meta's WhatsApp Business terms and policies, including getting people's permission before messaging them.
  • Meta charges your Organisation directly for WhatsApp messages. These charges are not included in CallerCRM prices.
  • If Meta restricts or blocks your number, we cannot restore it, and WhatsApp features will stop working until it is fixed with Meta.

Your data

  • The leads, call outcomes and messages your Organisation adds to CallerCRM belong to you. For this data, you decide what is collected and why, and we process it on your behalf only to provide CallerCRM.
  • You confirm you have a lawful basis, such as consent, to upload each lead's details and contact them.
  • Lead, call and WhatsApp records are deleted automatically after at most 20 days (up to 5 days later if copying to your export sheet fails), and from our encrypted backups within 7 days after that. Export anything you need to keep before then, for example by setting up an export sheet with us.
  • Your data is kept separate from every other Organisation's.
  • We never sell your data. Our internal admin panel shows us only totals about your activity, and every view is logged. Our staff look at other data only when needed to support you, fix a problem, or when the law requires it.
