Delete your CallerCRM account

CallerCRM by CodeByAkshay. Here's how to delete your account and data, what gets removed, and how long it takes.

On this page

  1. If you're an agent
  2. If you're an admin
  3. If an organisation called you
  4. What gets deleted

If you're an agent

Your account was created by your organisation's admin, so the quickest route is through them.

  1. Ask your admin to remove you. In the app they go to Manage Agents, open your profile and delete it. When an agent leaves, admins do this within 24–48 hours.
  2. Or email us at privacy@codebyakshay.com with the subject "CallerCRM account deletion". Include your username and the name of your organisation.
  3. We verify the request with your organisation and delete the account within 48 hours of verification. We confirm by email when it's done.

If you're an admin

  1. Email privacy@codebyakshay.com from the email address your organisation uses with us, with the subject "CallerCRM organisation deletion" and your admin username.
  2. We confirm the request with you, then delete your admin account, every agent account in your team and all your organisation's data.

If an organisation called you

If a business using CallerCRM contacted you and you want your details removed, ask that business first. It controls your data. You can also email privacy@codebyakshay.com with your phone number, and we will help get it deleted.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Account (name, username, password hash, phone number)Deleted permanently
Sign-in sessions and push-notification tokensDeleted permanently, and every device is signed out
Your lead assignments, call outcomes, notes and durationsDeleted permanently
Leads and WhatsApp messagesDeleted automatically within 20 days of being created, or straight away when a whole organisation is deleted
Server security logsKept briefly for security, then deleted
Encrypted nightly backupsDeleted automatically within 7 days, so deleted data leaves our backups within a week

Once deleted, data can't be recovered from the app. Encrypted backup copies are deleted automatically within 7 days. For full details, read our Privacy Policy.
