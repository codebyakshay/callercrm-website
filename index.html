Features

Everything your calling floor needs, in one app

Built around one loop: the admin assigns a lead, the agent calls it and marks the outcome. Everything else is there to make that loop faster.

One-tap native calling

Agents call straight from the lead card on their own SIM, so there's no VoIP lag and no extra telecom bill. Call length is captured automatically.

Outcome tracking

Interested, Callback, Called or Not Interested, plus a note, logged the moment the call ends. Every call builds up the lead's history.

Google Sheets import

Paste a sheet link, connect, and split the leads across your agents in seconds. You don't have to copy and paste anything.

WhatsApp inbox

Follow up with leads on the official WhatsApp Business Platform and read their replies without leaving the app.

Instant lead alerts

A push notification goes to the agent as soon as new leads are assigned to them, so fresh leads get called first.

Attendance & performance

Calls made, talk time and new interested leads for every agent, day by day. Present or absent shows up automatically.

Privacy & security

Your leads are your business, and nobody else's

We collect only what the app needs to work, keep it in India and delete it on a fixed schedule.

Hosted in India

Your data is stored on servers in India, close to your team.

Encrypted in transit

Everything between the app and our servers travels over HTTPS.

Hashed passwords

Passwords are never stored as plain text. Changing a password signs out every other device.

Role-based access

Agents see only their own leads, and admins see only their own team.

Short retention

Lead and call records are deleted automatically after 20 days at most.

No ads, no selling

We never sell data, never show ads and never record your calls.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which phones does CallerCRM work on?

CallerCRM is available for Android phones and is currently in closed testing on Google Play. Email us to join the test.

Do calls go through the internet or my SIM?

Your SIM. CallerCRM starts a normal phone call using your phone's dialer, so your usual carrier plan applies and call quality is the same as any other call.

Does CallerCRM record my calls?

No. CallerCRM never records audio and doesn't ask for microphone access. After a call to a lead, it reads only that call's length from your phone's call log.

Where is my data stored, and for how long?

On secure servers in India. Lead, call and WhatsApp records are deleted automatically after at most 20 days. When an agent leaves, their account is removed within 24–48 hours. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need to change how we use Google Sheets?

No. Keep your leads in the sheet you already use. Share it by link, paste the link into CallerCRM and assign the leads. CallerCRM never asks for access to your Google account.

How do I delete my account or data?

Ask your admin, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
